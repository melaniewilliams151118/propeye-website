Important Notice

PropEye conducts visual and operational inspections only. Our reports reflect the visible condition and basic operation of accessible fixtures, fittings, and appliances at the time of the inspection.

  • Visual inspections only
  • Basic operational checks
  • No electrical inspections
  • No plumbing inspections
  • No gas inspections
  • No structural, compliance or specialist technical inspections
