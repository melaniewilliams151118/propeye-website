Move-In & Move-Out Inspections Packages

PropEye Property Inspections provides professional, independent rental inspections for move-in and move-out properties. Our detailed inspections include comprehensive photographic evidence and professionally prepared reports to protect the landlord's investment, safeguard the tenant's deposit, and ensure a fair, transparent process for all parties.

Move-In Inspection

From R650

To accurately document the condition of the property before occupation begins.

  • Comprehensive room-by-room inspection
  • Detailed condition report
  • Photographic documentation
  • Testing of accessible light switches and plug points
  • Inspection of doors, windows, locks and gates
  • Inspection of cupboards, cabinets and built-in fixtures
  • Water & electricity meter readings
  • Checking for visible leaks, damp and mould
  • Identification of existing damage and maintenance concerns
  • Detailed digital inspection report
  • Report delivered within 24–48 hours

Move-Out Inspection

From R650

To accurately document the property's condition at the end of the occupation.

  • Comprehensive room-by-room inspection
  • Comparison against the move-in inspection (Requires a PropEye move-in inspection)
  • Photographic documentation
  • Testing of accessible light switches and plug points
  • Inspection of doors, windows, locks and gates
  • Inspection of cupboards, cabinets and built-in fixtures
  • Water & electricity meter readings
  • Identification of damages beyond normal wear and tear
  • Maintenance observations
  • Detailed digital inspection report
  • Report delivered within 24–48 hours

Larger Properties & Additional Inspection Time: For larger properties or inspections requiring additional time, an additional fee of R350 per hour applies. This may include large family homes, luxury properties, multiple outbuildings, or inspections requiring extensive photographic reporting.
